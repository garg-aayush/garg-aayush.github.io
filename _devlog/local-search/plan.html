Local search plan

Browser-local search for aayushgarg.dev, in Aayush's three stages, written for review. Rewritten 2026-11-11 from the journal (_devlog/local-search/journal.md), the scored runs in evals/results/ and two research files: research/2026-11-11-embedding-models.md (stage 2) and research/2026-11-11-answer-models.md (stage 3), where every model fact has its source. Updated 2026-11-12 with the stage 3 result: measured, not shipped. The page is live at /search/ as stages 1 and 2 (keyword plus meaning), from 2026-11-12.

  • Decided Aayush said so
  • Proposed the orchestrating session's recommendation, not yet approved
  • Open not settled
  • In progress being built now
  • Done built and measured

Search runs only in the visitor's browser, on /search/, in three stages: keyword search, then keyword plus meaning-based search, then a small language model that answers from the results (measured, not shipped).

Stage 1 is done: BM25 over a clean index raised recall at 5 from 0.33 to 0.75 on the 40 queries, with no model and a 117,508 byte download.

Stage 2 is built and measured in Chromium: BM25 plus a static table (potion-base-8M, int8, 256 dimensions, 5.70 MB with brotli, 7.95 MB raw) with fusion over the first 10 pages of each list raised recall at 5 from 0.75 to 0.89 and MRR at 10 from 0.64 to 0.77 on the 40 development queries, and on 20 held-out queries recall at 5 from 0.67 to 0.72 and nDCG at 10 from 0.60 to 0.67. The held-out set did not confirm the choice of fusion cut-off over fusing whole lists.

Stage 3 was measured and not shipped. Four small models (255 to 570 MB) answered 45 questions from four retrieved passages, labelled by two independent judges who agreed on 179 of 180 labels. The rule fixed in advance was to decline at least 13 of the 15 unanswerable questions and be faithful on at least 15 of 30 answerable ones; no model passes. The closest, Qwen3.5-0.8B, is faithful on 17 of 30 but declines 10 of 15; the other three decline 1 to 3 of 15. The site ships stages 1 and 2: keyword search plus the static embedding table. The harness, questions, labels and results stay in the repository.

The stages

Decided Aayush's three stages replaced the earlier five-stage proposal on 2026-11-11. Simplest first, each step measured before the next.

Stage 1: baseline, then BM25

Goal
Good keyword search on every device, with no model and no download beyond the index.
How
The page first ranked Quarto's search.json with a placeholder (the baseline). Then a post-render Node script writes a clean passage index from the rendered HTML (459 passages from 47 pages, tools included, sections split at 1,000 characters), and BM25 (k1 1.2, b 0.75, field weights 3, 2, 1, 0.5, Porter stemming, the last word as a prefix) runs in the browser as a module that Node runs unchanged for scoring.
Evaluated
Placeholder against 15 BM25 runs (stemming, prefix, index, three ablations) on the 40 queries. The default: recall at 5 of 0.75 against 0.33, MRR at 10 of 0.64 against 0.39, nDCG at 10 of 0.71 against 0.34, typing replay MRR 0.68 against 0.42.
Works
Lookup, topic, question and multi-page queries: recall at 5 of 1.00 in each type. Half-typed words. Ranking takes 0.028 ms per query (median) and preparing the index 17.9 ms, in Chromium on a laptop.
Does not
9 of 36 answerable queries need meaning: five paraphrases ("patching holes in a chart that repeats every year"), a tool by its effect ("make a photo black and white"), two person questions ("what does he do now", "has he worked in oil and gas") and the British spelling "diarisation". The four no-answer queries return 28, 31, 9 and 2 pages.
Fallback
Titles and descriptions only, if the index cannot be read.
Exit test
Met: one variant wired into the page with tests, and every run in the journal.
Status
Done The page address and entry points are Decided (/search/, the navbar magnifier replacing Quarto's overlay, / from any page, a link on the Writing page); it went live on 2026-11-12 (journal entry "2026-11-12, going live").

Stage 2: keyword plus semantic search

Goal
Find the queries BM25 misses by meaning, without losing the ones it finds, at the smallest download that does it.
How
Embed the 506 passages (459 plus the 47 page descriptions) at build time and only the query in the browser, with the same static table; fuse with BM25 by reciprocal rank fusion over the first 10 pages of each list. Measured offline in Node first, on the same 40 queries, then built and measured in the browser.
Evaluated and compared with
BM25 alone, and each model alone and fused with BM25: minishlab/potion-base-8M (a static table, no runtime), Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (included at Aayush's request), MongoDB/mdbr-leaf-ir, and EmbeddingGemma 2 as the reference for what a large model would add. Per type and per query wins and losses, with the download.
Works
BM25 plus the potion table (int8, 256 dims) fixes four queries BM25 misses ("paraphrase-04", "paraphrase-05", "partial-03" the spelling "diarisation", "person-02" oil and gas) and breaks only "question-04", the weather page, which has no passages to embed; with the 47 page descriptions embedded it breaks none. Recall at 5 0.75 to 0.85, MRR at 10 0.64 to 0.75, nDCG at 10 0.71 to 0.79; paraphrase recall 0.33 to 0.67, partial 0.60 to 0.80, person 0.50 to 0.75.
Does not
No hybrid finds "tool-02" (make a photo black and white) or "person-03", although every transformer alone ranks them in the top 4 (see the fusion caveat). MiniLM fixes nothing over potion and breaks nothing; leaf-ir fixes "paraphrase-01" and breaks "paraphrase-06"; EmbeddingGemma 2 at 768 fixes nothing; so none passes the adoption rule.
Caveats
  • Every model alone has higher recall at 5 than the same model fused with BM25 (leaf-ir 0.96 against 0.85, Gemma 0.98 against 0.85, MiniLM 0.94 against 0.85, potion 0.87 against 0.85). Reciprocal rank fusion of whole lists lets BM25's long tail of word matches outrank pages only the model finds. The fusion method matters and should be settled on queries other than these 40.
  • Under a different fusion (only the first 10 rows of each list), leaf-ir would fix "paraphrase-01" and "person-03" over potion and break nothing (with descriptions), passing the adoption rule at about four times the download.
  • Differences of one or two queries are noise at 36 answerable queries; one query moves the overall score by about 0.03.
  • Cosine scores alone do not separate answerable from unanswerable queries (AUC 0.66 to 0.83; a cutoff that silences the four no-answer queries keeps the right page for only 13 to 23 of 36), so stage 3 needs a second signal for its gate.
  • EmbeddingGemma 2 at 256 dims dropped recall at 5 alone from 0.98 to 0.86 (four queries), against its card's "close to lossless"; unexplained.
  • The JavaScript reimplementation was compared with Model2Vec 0.10.0 on 580 texts: identical token ids, float32 cosine 1.000000000, int8 against float32 at least 0.9989 on queries and passages.
  • On 20 held-out queries the fusion cut-off did not beat fusing whole lists (recall at 5 0.72 against 0.78, same nDCG), though it did on the development set (0.89 against 0.86); 18 queries cannot settle it.
Fallback
BM25 results show at once and stay while the table loads. A failed load, a hash mismatch or vectors for another index: keyword search, status unchanged, no message. Tested with the table blocked, the vectors blocked, vectors of another index and a table with one byte changed.
Exit test
A model ships only if it fixes queries BM25 misses without breaking ones it gets; ties go to the smaller download. Met by the potion table offline and in the browser (development: five queries fixed, none broken; held-out: one fixed, none broken).
Built
BM25 plus potion-base-8M as an int8 table at 256 dimensions, fused by reciprocal rank fusion (k 60) over the first 10 pages of each list, loaded on first use of the search box without asking. Passage vectors are made on every render by scripts/build-search-vectors.mjs from the committed table in static/search/: no model download, no npm install and no cache in the deploy. npm run check:site fails if the index or the vectors miss a page or do not match. Adding or editing a post needs no search step (shown end to end in results/stage2-browser/e2e-new-post.md); a page that is not a post needs one entry in search/index.qmd.
Browser test results
(1) The browser's query vector matches Node's for 60 queries (cosine 1.000000000) and the rankings are identical. (2) The three files are 7,952,080 bytes raw and 5,697,447 with brotli; ready 32.5 ms after the first focus locally, 4.7 s at 10 Mbit/s and 29 s at 1.6 Mbit/s with brotli; typing stays responsive (0.36 ms per keystroke through the whole handler). Phone and GitHub Pages' compression not measured. (3) A failed or mismatched load leaves keyword search working. (4) Per-query time on a phone was not measured; in Chromium on a laptop 0.11 ms. (5) In a Chromium profile with a disk cache a second visit within the max-age made no request; a changed index or table has a new URL; other browsers not checked. (6) Typing: the first page changes about once per typed word against 0.47 for BM25, and the replay MRR equals BM25's (0.68); the flicker is in the first rows.
Status
Done built, measured in Chromium and live. Open: the fusion cut-off (held-out disagrees), what to show for queries the site cannot answer, phone and real network.

Stage 3: answers from the search results

Goal
A short answer, with numbered sources, from the passages search found, generated in the visitor's browser, only when the visitor asks for it.
How
Retrieve the four best passages (stage 2's ranking, or BM25), skip the model if nothing shares a content word with the question, then prompt: use only these numbered passages, at most two sentences, cite passage numbers, or say the site does not seem to cover it. Transformers.js in a Web Worker, WebGPU (with shader-f16) or single-threaded WebAssembly, greedy decoding, no repetition penalty, streamed, stopped after two sentences. The passages always show below the answer, numbered and linked. Model files at a pinned revision from huggingface.co; the runtime self-hosted.
Evaluated
Four small models, all at q4f16 on WebGPU in Chromium on one laptop (Apple M5 Pro), greedy decoding, one prompt: Qwen3-0.6B, IBM Granite 4.0 350M (added by Aayush), LiquidAI LFM2.5-350M and Qwen3.5-0.8B text. 45 questions written before any model ran: 30 answerable and 15 the site does not answer (8 near misses on topics the site covers, 7 off-topic). Each question was typed into the page's own hybrid ranking and the top four pages were passed, one passage per page. Two independent judges (Opus 5.5 subagents) labelled all 180 answers faithful, unsupported or non-answer against the passages given, blind to the model and reading in opposite orders. They agreed on 179 of 180 labels; the one difference is Granite on "how does DPO get away without a reward model", which changes Granite's faithful count from 10 to 11 and nothing else. Rule fixed in advance: decline at least 13 of the 15 unanswerable questions and be faithful on at least 15 of the 30 answerable ones, both at once.

How it works

The diagrams could not load (the Mermaid script comes from cdn.jsdelivr.net). The text under each caption describes them.

At build time

Everything the browser needs for search is computed when the site is rendered and published as static files next to the pages. Answer models are not built; they are downloaded as published.

On a keystroke

BM25 is a pure function from the query and the index to ranked passages, grouped to one row per page. Semantic search adds a second path that runs only when its model is ready.

Asking for an answer

Not built: this is the design the stage 3 evaluation tested, kept as the record, because no model passed the rule (see stage 3). It would have run only after the visitor pressed Ask, with the results already on the page and staying there whatever happened.

What the page would have checked before offering a model (not built). The same checks for all four candidates; there was no separate tier for capable devices. requestAdapter() returns an adapter that is not a fallback and has shader-f16 (for q4f16 weights), else WebAssembly; WebGPU-only models are not offered on WebAssembly. navigator.storage.estimate() has room. The files are already cached (then no download). An earlier load did not end unfinished. It cannot see free GPU memory, and iOS may reload a page under memory pressure with no error. Downloads live in Cache Storage (transformers-cache) until the visitor presses Delete, or the browser evicts the site under storage pressure. Details and sources are in the two research files.

Keeping the index current

Done for the keyword index: the post-render script rebuilds it on every quarto render, locally and in the deploy (quarto-publish.yml, on every push to master), with Node built-ins only, so a new or edited post is searchable on the next publish. Proposed for stage 2 vectors: cached by a hash of each passage's text, heading and title for one exact model, so only new or changed passages are embedded on the runner's CPU. Answer models need nothing at build time.

The manifest records the model id and revision, quantization, dimension, prefixes and a probe vector (the embedding of a fixed sentence). The browser embeds the same sentence after loading and uses keyword search only if the result differs beyond a set tolerance. At 459 passages the vectors are small: 117,504 bytes at int8 and 256 dimensions, 352,512 at 768.

Models

The stage 3 evaluation of 2026-11-12 measured all four answer models and none passed the rule; the notes below were written before it and are kept as written, apart from what it settled. Checked on 2026-11-11 against each model's card and file listing. Stage 2 candidates, sizes, licenses and benchmarks: embedding research. Stage 3 candidates (LiquidAI, Qwen, Gemma, SmolLM, Granite, Phi, Llama, Pleias), runtimes, hosting, caching and the similar site's method and numbers: answer model research. Each file ends with what could not be verified.

  • Why Qwen3-0.6B is tested first. Of the four, it has the best published numbers on a comparable site (18 of 30 faithful, 6 of 15 invented), it is Apache 2.0, and it runs on WebAssembly as well as WebGPU. Sizes of onnx-community/Qwen3-0.6B-ONNX: 583 MB at q4f16 (needs shader-f16 on WebGPU), 631 MB at q8, 933 MB at q4; Qwen's own Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B-GGUF is 639 MB at Q8_0, for wllama. Its weakness is the one that matters most here: it still invents answers.
  • Why Granite 4.0 350M is second. Aayush added it on 2026-11-11. Of the small models with no published grounded-answering numbers, it is the one whose publisher lists RAG and question answering among its capabilities (its RAG example says "Coming soon"), it is Apache 2.0, and at 357 MB (q4f16; 576 MB at q4) it is between LFM2.5-350M and Qwen3-0.6B. The ONNX repository is tagged for Transformers.js; on 2026-11-12 it loaded and generated on WebGPU and on single threaded WebAssembly. Context 32,768 tokens.
  • Why LiquidAI is third. Aayush prefers LiquidAI, so it was weighed first. LiquidAI's card for LFM2.5-350M recommends it for extraction and tool use and "not ... for knowledge-intensive tasks"; on another personal blog it invented answers for 13 of 15 unanswerable questions; and it runs only on WebGPU. It is the smallest download and stays on the list to be measured here.
  • Ruled out by Aayush on 2026-11-11. The similar site's fine-tune bartdegoede/qwen3-0.6b-small-rag; for context only, that site ships it as its default and reports 16 of 30 faithful and 0 of 15 invented on its own questions. And any larger on-demand answer model (LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct, LFM2-1.2B-RAG). Also considered and not taken up: Pleias-RAG-350M.

How stages are compared

Decided The 40-query set in evals/questions.json (36 answerable, 4 with no answer, nine types), used unchanged at every stage, and every measurement recorded in the journal.

Proposed The rules:

  • The same queries, page list and index at every stage. Ranking runs in Node with the module the browser uses.
  • Results per query type with recall at 5, MRR at 10 and nDCG at 10, plus what comes back for the no-answer queries (count and top score).
  • One query moves a per-type score by 17 to 25 points, so every comparison also lists per-query wins and losses.
  • A heavier configuration is adopted only if it fixes queries the lighter one misses without breaking ones it got right. Ties go to the smaller download.
  • Cost is reported next to quality every time: download, time until usable, time per query, memory; on a laptop, and on a phone once there is something to run on one.

Answers (stage 3)

Search scores cannot judge an answer, so stage 3 had its own set and labels. Run on 2026-11-12; the scores are in the stage 3 block above and the tables in the journal entry "2026-11-12, stage 3: result and decision" and from score.mjs with the two labels files, labels-a.json and labels-b.json.

  • Questions. 45 in evals/answers/questions.json: 30 answerable (12 fact, 8 how, 6 broad, 4 person) and 15 the site does not answer (8 near misses, 7 off-topic), written before any model ran and without looking at retrieval.
  • Labels. Faithful (every claim is in the passages), unsupported (a claim is not, even if true) or non-answer (a decline), against the passages the model was given. On the unanswerable half a non-answer is the right result.
  • Same input. The four passages were fixed and stored per question, one passage per page, so every model read the same text; greedy decoding.
  • Judges. Two independent Opus 5.5 subagents, blind to the model and reading in opposite orders; 179 of 180 labels agree. No human label is recorded.
  • Gate. The "nothing shares a content word" gate would have fired on 0 of the 15 unanswerable questions, so it removes nothing; the model's own decline was the only signal and it was weak.
  • Cost. Download, first token and speed on WebGPU on a laptop, and which models load on WebAssembly (stage 3 block). No phone.

Questions for Aayush

Ordered by how much each answer unblocks. One recommendation each.

  1. Revisit answers later? Nothing ships now, and the data says the missing skill is declining: of 60 answers to unanswerable questions, 43 were answers and all unsupported. Recommended: not now. If you want to try again, these are the possibilities, none planned, each with what the evidence here says:
    • Pass more than one passage per page, or more passages. Supported as a fix for retrieval (9 of the 10 misses had the right page in the top four); not shown to fix the models, which failed on hit questions too (best 13 of 20 faithful).
    • A retrieval score or an agreement signal for the gate. Evidence is against a score (stage 2: cosine does not separate answerable from unanswerable; the content-word gate fired on 0 of 15); no agreement signal was measured. A score gate can be tested offline on the stored retrieval without running a model.
    • A larger model, or one trained to decline. You ruled both out on 2026-11-11. Another blog's counts (a decline-trained fine-tune: 16 of 30 faithful and 0 of 15 invented; Gemma 4 E2B: 28 and 0) point that way but were not measured here.
    • A stricter prompt. One prompt was tried; Qwen3.5 declined 14 of 45 in its own words while Granite and LFM2.5 almost never declined, so wording may matter. Untested.
  2. Approve BM25 plus the potion table as the stage 2 choice? It fixed four queries offline and broke none once the weather page's description is embedded; no heavier model passed the rule. Recommended: yes, then start the browser test.
  3. May a table of about 5.6 MB compressed (8.2 MB raw) load without asking, on first use of the search box on the search page only? Recommended: yes; if that is too much, the 128 dimension table (3.1 MB compressed, one query worse).
  4. Review the six search judgments listed in evals/README.md; every stage 1 and 2 score depends on them.
